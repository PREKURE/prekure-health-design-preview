Is this for you?

Sound familiar?

  • “I don’t feel like myself. I feel like I’m losing the plot.”
  • “I’m awake at 3am, then carrying a full day on no sleep.”
  • “I was told to go on HRT. After that, I was on my own.”
  • “I’m holding up a job, a family and everyone else. There’s nothing left over for me.”

It’s probably not for you if

  • You need urgent or emergency care. Call 111.
  • You want someone to prescribe or change your medicines. That stays with your prescriber.
  • You’re under 18.

Your GP stays in charge of your medical care. Coaching works alongside it, never instead of it.

How you’ll know it’s working.

Agree the symptom or daily-life outcome you want to track. Review it with your coach using a recognised symptom measure, with a progress review partway through and an outcome review at the end. You also consider sleep, strength and how you feel.
