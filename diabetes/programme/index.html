Is this for you?

Sound familiar?

  • “I left the appointment with a pamphlet and a follow-up in three months.”
  • “I know what I’m supposed to eat. Doing it every day, in my house, is another thing.”
  • “They said ‘prediabetes’, gave me a leaflet, and told me they’d re-test in a year.”
  • “I was told it only gets worse. Part of me doesn’t accept that.”

It’s probably not for you if

  • You need urgent or emergency care. Call 111.
  • You want someone to prescribe or change your medicines. That stays with your prescriber.
  • You’re under 18.

PREKURE 30-Day Accountability Guarantee.

Show up, engage, and give the process a fair go for one month. If you then decide the programme isn’t right for you, we’ll refund you. This is a promise about participation and fit - not a promise of a specific result in one month.

Your GP stays in charge of your medical care. Coaching works alongside it, never instead of it.

How you’ll know it’s working.

Agree what to track with your coach and GP. HbA1c results come from the blood tests your GP arranges. You also review your everyday habits and how you feel, with a progress review partway through and an outcome review at the end.
