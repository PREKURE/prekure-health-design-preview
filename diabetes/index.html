What is a PREKURE Coach?

A Certified PREKURE Coach trained in lifestyle medicine and behaviour change. Your coach helps you set practical goals, plan actions and review progress through sessions and check-ins. Coaching works alongside your GP and healthcare team.

How you’ll know it’s working.

Agree what to track with your coach and GP. Your GP continues to monitor your HbA1c. Your coach focuses on the daily habits you choose. You also review your everyday habits and how you feel, with a progress review partway through and an outcome review at the end.
