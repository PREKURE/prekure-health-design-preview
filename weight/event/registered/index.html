Who is the PREKURE Clinic?

New Zealand’s lifestyle-first clinic. We believe prevention is cure, and we bring 30 years of research and practice to helping you get to what’s behind it, not just the symptoms.

  • Research and practice under one roof

    Professors, PhD researchers, practising clinicians and certified health coaches, translating the science into everyday life.

  • One-on-one coaching

    A certified PREKURE Coach works with you over 6 to 12 weeks, online, anywhere in New Zealand.

  • A PREKURE Doctor, when you need one

    Opening soon. Lifestyle first, alongside your coaching and your current GP.
