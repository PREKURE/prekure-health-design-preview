Want the basics first? GLP-1s and coaching, explained

What is a GLP-1 medicine?

A prescription medicine used for weight management and type 2 diabetes. It works partly by reducing appetite. Whether one is right for you is a decision for you and your prescriber.

What does a PREKURE Coach do?

A Certified PREKURE Coach is trained in lifestyle medicine and behaviour change. Your coach helps you set goals, plan practical actions and review progress, alongside your usual healthcare.
