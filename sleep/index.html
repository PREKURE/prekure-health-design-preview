Coaching has a clear boundary

Some sleep problems need clinical care.

A PREKURE Coach does not diagnose or treat insomnia, sleep apnoea or another sleep disorder, and does not start, stop or change sleep medicine. Coaching can help with everyday routines and behaviour while a GP or sleep clinician handles diagnosis and treatment.

Loud snoring, gasping or pauses in breathing, or sleepiness that makes driving or work unsafe, belong with a GP first.

Regular support: Your coach helps you choose practical actions and reviews how they went at your next session.

Scheduled check-ins give you time to ask questions and adjust the plan between sessions.

What is a PREKURE Coach?

A Certified PREKURE Coach trained in lifestyle medicine and behaviour change. Your coach helps you set practical goals, plan actions and review progress through sessions and check-ins. Coaching works alongside your GP and healthcare team.
