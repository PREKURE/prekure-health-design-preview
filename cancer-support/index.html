Supportive care, clearly bounded

Your clinical team stays in charge of cancer care.

A PREKURE Coach does not diagnose cancer, interpret scans, recommend treatments, advise on medicines or prescribe a cancer diet. Coaching helps with day-to-day routines, pacing, sleep, movement, nourishment, stress and follow-through within the advice your clinicians have given you.

New, severe, rapidly worsening or unexpected symptoms belong with your cancer team, GP, Healthline or emergency care - not a coaching questionnaire.

Regular support: Your coach helps you choose practical actions and reviews how they went at your next session.

Scheduled check-ins give you time to ask questions and adjust the plan between sessions.

What is a PREKURE Coach?

A PREKURE Certified Health Coach: trained in lifestyle medicine and behaviour-change science, through a curriculum built by professors, doctors and health professionals. They’re the person who helps you turn what you know into what you actually do - alongside your healthcare team, never instead of it.

The goal isn’t to need us forever; it’s to get your health back in your hands.

Wherever you are with it.

Diagnosis and planning

When life has narrowed to appointments and decisions, coaching can help you protect capacity, prepare questions and keep the basics workable. Treatment decisions stay with your clinicians.

During treatment

Build flexible routines around treatment days, recovery, nourishment, movement and the people who depend on you - always within your clinical guidance.

After treatment

Finishing treatment is not the same as feeling finished. Coaching can support confidence, identity, strength, work and a new version of ordinary life.

Ongoing or advanced cancer

The focus is what matters to you now: energy, connection, meaning and making the week more manageable alongside specialist and palliative care where involved.

Symptoms and treatment questions go to your clinical team first.

Contact your cancer team or GP about anything new, severe, rapidly worsening or different from what you were told to expect. Call Healthline on 0800 611 116 for health advice, or 111 in an emergency.

Coaching cannot control cancer or promise a treatment outcome. It can give you a steady person, a realistic next step and support to make the life around care more manageable.

How you’ll know support is helping.

You and your coach choose a day-to-day outcome that matters to you - perhaps making recovery days more workable, protecting sleep, moving within your clinical guidance, returning to one valued activity or feeling less alone with the plan. You review it together and adjust without blame. Tumour markers, scans and treatment response are measured and interpreted only by your clinical team.
