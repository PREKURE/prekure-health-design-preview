What is a PREKURE Burnout & Stress Coach?

A Certified PREKURE Health Coach with a special interest in mental fitness, trained in lifestyle medicine and behaviour change. Your coach helps you plan manageable actions around stress, recovery and daily routines, alongside your healthcare team.

Choose your goals and practise changes you can continue after the programme.

Wherever you are with it.

Stretched and stressed

Look at your current pressures and choose manageable changes with your coach.

Flat and stuck

Close to burnout

Work on recovery and boundaries around chronic workplace stress. Some changes will need support from your workplace or healthcare team.

When you need mental health care.

Coaching is for building capacity when you’re safe and stable. If you’re in crisis, coaching isn’t the right support - a doctor or mental health professional is. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 111. For free support any time, call or text 1737.

A PREKURE Burnout & Stress Coach is a PREKURE Certified Health Coach with a special interest in mental fitness: trained through a curriculum built by professors, doctors and health professionals in lifestyle medicine and behaviour-change science. They’re not a therapist, a counsellor or a personal trainer.
